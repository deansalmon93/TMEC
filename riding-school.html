Our Riding School

Lessons for every level

Whether you're a nervous first-timer or an experienced rider looking to sharpen your competition skills, our coaches will tailor each lesson to you. Our horses and ponies are chosen to suit riders of every size and ability.

With a 60m × 24m indoor arena, lessons go ahead whatever the Irish weather throws at us.

  • Children from age 6
  • Adult beginners and returning riders
  • Group and private (one-to-one) lessons
  • Dressage, show jumping and cross-country coaching

Lessons

Choose your lesson

Children's Lessons

Fun, safe and structured lessons for young riders from age six, building balance, confidence and a love of ponies.

Adult Beginners

It's never too late to start. Patient coaching on steady horses for adults new to riding or coming back to it.

Group Lessons

Ride alongside others of a similar level. A sociable way to progress and a great place to start.

Private Lessons

One-to-one sessions focused entirely on you, ideal for nervous riders or for working on something specific.

Cross-Country Lessons

Learn to ride across country on our 1½-mile course, with fences and water complexes to suit every level.

Competition Coaching

Improve your dressage, show jumping and cross-country with coaches who have competed up to Novice eventing.

Getting Started

Your first lesson

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

1. Book

Call 028 4481 1880 or send us an enquiry. We'll find the right lesson for you.

2. Dress for riding

Long trousers, boots with a small heel and a riding hat. Hats can be borrowed. Please ask when booking.

3. Arrive early

Come 15 minutes before your lesson so we can get you fitted and introduced to your horse or pony.

4. Enjoy the ride

Your coach will take things at your pace. Most of all, we want you to have fun.
