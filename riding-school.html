Riding School

A BHS Approved Riding Centre offering lessons to suit all levels, for riders aged 6 and above.

Learn to ride

Lessons for everyone

As a British Horse Society Approved Riding Centre, our experienced instructors offer lessons to suit all levels, from complete beginners to competing riders, aged 6 and above.

Lessons take place in our international-size indoor arena, so they go ahead whatever the weather. As riders progress they can move on to our large outdoor facilities, including full show jumping and cross-country courses. All riding equipment is provided.

  • BHS Approved Riding Centre
  • Lessons to suit all levels, from age 6
  • Group and private lessons
  • Weekday evening lessons for experienced riders
  • All equipment provided
Book a lesson

Our lessons

Age 6+

Children's lessons

Group lessons that build confidence, balance and horse sense, on ponies matched to each child's size and ability. Ideal for complete beginners and improving young riders.

Beginner to experienced

Adult lessons

Whether you're starting out, returning after a break or looking to improve your jumping, our adult groups run in the evenings during the week. An enjoyable way to get fit.

One-to-one

Private lessons

Focused, individual tuition at your own pace. Choose a 30 or 45 minute session to work on whatever matters most to you.

Experienced riders

Cross-country schooling

Put your skills to the test over our full cross-country course of around 40 fences, including water, with an instructor alongside to help you and your horse grow in confidence.
