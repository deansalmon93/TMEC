Since 1998

Autumn Two Phase Event

Our annual Autumn Two Phase is a highlight of the season and has been running since 1998. Classes range from 65cm for younger riders up to 1 metre, with a course that incorporates Eventing Ireland and Pony Club cross-country obstacles.

Riders compete for trophies including the Tullymurry Perpetual Trophy, the Tullymurry Junior Event Trophy and the Becky McKay Memorial Trophy.

Qualifiers

RDS Dublin Horse Show qualifiers

Tullymurry has hosted qualifiers for the RDS Dublin Horse Show, including the Young Event Horse Series (4 year old, 5 year old and Small Event Horse) and the Performance Irish Draught and Connemara Performance Hunter Championship classes.

We also host Eventing Ireland Northern Region competitions on courses built by our own team.

Volunteers

Our events run on teamwork

Every competition at Tullymurry relies on scorers, fence judges, show jump judges, arena parties, call-ups, time keepers and many more volunteers.

Our cross-country course is built and presented to a high standard by David O'Connor and the team, and we're grateful to St John Ambulance and the paramedics who keep everyone safe. If you'd like to help at a future event, we'd love to hear from you.
