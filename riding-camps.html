Riding Camps

Days full of ponies, riding and new friends. Our holiday camps are the highlight of the year for young riders.

Holiday fun

Unforgettable days in the saddle

Our riding camps give young riders the chance to spend whole days with the horses, learning, riding and having fun.

Campers ride every day, learn to care for their pony, and have the run of our arenas and paddock under the watchful eye of our instructors. It's a brilliant way to build confidence, improve riding and make friends who love horses as much as they do.

What to expect

A day at camp

  • Riding lessons in the arenas, matched to each child's ability
  • Grooming, tacking up and learning to care for ponies
  • Stable management and horse knowledge sessions
  • Mounted games and fun competitions
  • Supervised by experienced, friendly staff throughout
Good to know

What to bring

Clothing

Long trousers or jodhpurs, boots with a small heel, and layers including a waterproof jacket. Riding hats are provided, or bring your own if it meets current safety standards.

Food & drink

A packed lunch, snacks and a refillable water bottle. Please let us know about any allergies when booking.
