Camps at Tullymurry

Riding, friends & fun

Our riding camps run during the school holidays and are a brilliant way for young riders to build skills and confidence and make new friends. Riders are grouped by age and ability so everyone gets the most from their day.

With our indoor arena, camps carry on whatever the weather.

  • Daily riding sessions with qualified coaches
  • Stable management: grooming, tacking up and pony care
  • Games, quizzes and fun activities
  • Suitable for beginners through to confident riders

Be Prepared

What to bring

  • Riding hat (to current safety standard). Hats can be borrowed. Please ask when booking.
  • Boots with a small heel, or jodhpur boots
  • Jodhpurs or comfortable long trousers
  • Waterproof jacket and layers for the weather
  • Packed lunch, snacks and a drink
  • Sun cream in the summer

Please let us know about any medical conditions or allergies when you book.

FAQs

Camp questions

What age are camps for?

Camps are for young riders from age six. Riders are grouped by age and ability.

Does my child need riding experience?

No. Complete beginners are very welcome, and we'll make sure they're on a suitable pony with a coach at their level.

How do I book a place?

Call us on 028 4481 1880 or send an enquiry through our contact page. Places are limited, so book early.

How much does camp cost?

Please contact us for current camp prices and dates.
