Livery

Keep your horse at our family-run yard, with access to some of the best facilities in County Down.

A home for your horse

Livery at Tullymurry

Our liveries are part of the Tullymurry family, and many help out at our events alongside our own team.

Ours is a friendly, family-run yard looked after by people who know and love horses, at a BHS Approved centre. Liveries can make full use of our international-size indoor arena and large outdoor facilities, including full show jumping and cross-country courses.

  • Stabling at a friendly, family-run yard
  • International-size indoor arena for year-round riding
  • Large outdoor facilities
  • Full show jumping and cross-country courses on site
  • Lessons and schooling with our instructors
