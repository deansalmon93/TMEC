Welcome

Welcome to Tullymurry Equestrian Centre

Tullymurry is a family-run equestrian centre just outside Downpatrick, set around eighteenth-century courtyard stables. For three generations we have taught riders of every age and ability, kept horses at livery and hosted some of the best-loved events in the Northern Ireland calendar.

As a British Horse Society Approved Riding Centre, our experienced, friendly instructors offer lessons to suit all levels for riders aged six and above. With an international-size indoor arena and large outdoor facilities, including full show jumping and cross-country courses, there is somewhere to ride whatever the weather. We are also proud to be affiliated with East Down Pony Club and to be a local base for Riding for the Disabled.
