Welcome to Tullymurry

A friendly yard with serious facilities

Tullymurry Equestrian Centre sits in the rolling countryside just outside Downpatrick. Whether you're sitting on a pony for the very first time or schooling for your next event, you'll find patient coaching, well-schooled horses and ponies, and the space to progress.

Our team brings over twenty years of experience coaching and competing up to Novice level eventing. We're a British Horse Society training and exam centre, and a local base for Riding for the Disabled and the Pony Club.
