Clubs

Tullymurry is proud to be affiliated with East Down Pony Club and to support riders of all abilities.

The Pony Club

Affiliated with East Down Pony Club

We are proud to be affiliated with East Down Pony Club, a branch of The Pony Club in Area 17, Northern Ireland.

The Pony Club gives young riders the chance to learn about horses and riding, take part in rallies, camps and competitions, and work towards Pony Club achievement badges and tests, all while making friends who share their love of ponies.

If your child rides with us and would like to join, we're happy to point you in the right direction.
